> TLDR; We should define "Full Battery" as 80% full, with an option to occasionally "Super Charge" (or something like that) up to 100%.
> This makes battery longevity behavior the default and re-frames full charging as a bonus, rather than charge limiting as a sacrifice.

For the past six months I have set all of my devices that support it to limit charging to between 70-80%.

I started this experiment because (1) I heard a few times that it was good for battery health and (2) I had to replace my last phone due to declining battery health after just 2 years.
