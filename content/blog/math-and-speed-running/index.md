In a recent interview between Hank Green and Brian Sanders (3Blue1Brown), Sanders mentioned his theory that given where LLMs are and their rate of progress its likely that finding mathematical proofs won't be a "First Past the Post" problem. Finding proofs will be relatively easy, the hard part will be finding meaning and conveying that to everybody else. What's important stops being who proves something first, but rather who, or what combination of people, understand it best.

I really like this point and it reminded me of speed running. I'm not an active participant in speed running, but I'm a fan of the community and especially like [this video by Marblr about a Portal glitch and its impact on Portal speed running](https://youtu.be/a9c4jrgfhsk).
