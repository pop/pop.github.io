"AI" companies train their models on pretty much all data on the internet.
Every post, comment, picture, video, file -- anything you can think of.

Now if this was being created and given to the masses for free, that'd be one thing.
Your work was stolen, but now you get to use this powerful tool at no charge.
Not ideal, I didn't ask for my art to be put into the everything engine, but there it is.

But no.
Of course it's not free to use.
If you want to use the everything engine, which contains everything you ever did online, you need to pay.
Maybe not pay with a subscription, but pay with ads or lost revenue to your own site.
