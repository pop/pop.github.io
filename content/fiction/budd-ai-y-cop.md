JJ got to her desk at the precinct at 7:30.

<!-- Describe the day, location, commute -->

The office wasn't empty but at least she beat the morning rush.
Probably had an hour or two to get real work before meetings and other riffraff created a productivity black-hole in her day between the hours of 10am and 6pm.

JJ used to work from home as a blue-hoodie coder but traded in the laptop for a badge five years ago.
At the time it made sense, gave her life a sense of purpose, helping the people in the city she lived in her whole life.
Unfortunately the institution of law enforcement had a habit of wringing that aspiration out of recruits.
Now all that was left was an over-worked, over-caffeinated, and under-slept mess they called a detective.

<!-- OK but why _did_ she choose this career? -->

At least she could say she was part of a union.
Never got to say that in the private sector.

A direct message from her boss, Sgt. Raymond, popped up just as JJ sat at her desk.
She had turned notifications off for almost everybody in the instant messaging client, all but one.
<!-- why? -->
It read:

> Ray (07:32):
> JJ you're being assigned a new partner.
> Check your Codec.

At the risk of sounding cliche JJ really did not want a partner right now.
Her last partner was caught smuggling illegal fruit into the country and despite that sounding pretty tame got them fired.
JJ wasn't involved but it's really hard to convince an internal investigation that the person you work with 8 hours a day 5 days a week keep secrets from you.
So trust wasn't super high and frankly she just didn't want to meet new people.

JJ took another sip of coffee as she contemplated a response when her Codec started to buzz.
She didn't check her work Codec much, just when she had to like taking photos of crime scenes and paying for work related expenses.
It did technically function as a note taker, chat device, but JJ was an old soul and preferred to do those with a keyboard and mouse, even if everybody in the office resented her clicks and clacks; most of them had headphones in all day anyway why did they care?
