<details><summary>🪐 martian chess♟️</summary>
<h2>martian chess</h2>

![martian chess gameplay](/images/games/martian-chess.gif)

* free (download): [liquidmirror.itch.io/martian-chess]
* tools: [bevy] (engine), [fmod] (audio), [blender] (visuals)
* developed: TIMEFRAME, released: DATE
* visuals and music by [Sam Hall]

After failing for more than a year to make progess on an original game idea, I brought the idea of implementing [Martian Chess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martian_chess) to Sam.
The idea being the game design was "done" and the value we were adding was appealing visuals and music.
Sam agreed and we spent a few months making on our first finished game: Martian Chess.
We contacted the publishers of the original board game and let them know of the project; they agreed to let us develop the game with the caveat that we not make money from it.

[liquidmirror.itch.io/martian-chess]: https://liquidmirror.itch.io/martian-chess
</details>

<details><summary>🎴 set</summary>
<h2>set</h2>

![set gameplay](/images/games/set.gif)

* free (in-browser): [popgame.itch.io/set](https://popgame.itch.io/set)
* tools: [bevy] (engine), [rx] (visuals)
* developed: TIMEFRAME, released: DATE
* music by [Sam Hall]

For a house art show I put together this very rudimentary single-player version of [Set!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_(card_game)) and setup a laptop in the corner for people to play.
Every single introvert played a few rounds and gave me some great feedback.

[popgame.itch.io/set]: https://popgame.itch.io/set
</details>

<details><summary>🐦 flappy bird (with rewind)⏳</summary>
<h2>flappy bird (with rewind)</h2>

![flappy bird (with rewind) gameplay](/images/games/flappy.gif)

* free (in-browser): [popgame.itch.io/flappy](https://popgame.itch.io/flappy)
* tools: [bevy] (engine), [avian] (physics), [blender] (visuals)
* developed: TIMEFRAME, released: DATE

Around June of 2025 I decided to focus on making many small games by copying existing ideas and adding a fun twist.
[Flappy Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flappy_Bird) came to mind as a simple game to start with, and because I was playing [Braid, Anniversery Edition](https://store.steampowered.com/app/499180/Braid_Anniversary_Edition/) the idea of using rewind in some way came about naturally.
I was especially interested because rewind seemed easy to implement given the limited mechanics and I was genuinely curious if it would make the game more or less fun.

[popgame.itch.io/flappy]: https://popgame.itch.io/flappy
</details>
