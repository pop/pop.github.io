* tools: [bevy] (engine), [avian] (physics), [blender] (visuals)
* developed: july - august 2025, released: september 2025

Around June of 2025 I decided to focus on making many small games by copying existing ideas and adding a fun twist.
[Flappy Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flappy_Bird) came to mind as a simple game to start with, and because I was playing [Braid, Anniversery Edition](https://store.steampowered.com/app/499180/Braid_Anniversary_Edition/) the idea of using rewind in some way came about naturally.
I was especially interested because rewind seemed easy to implement given the limited mechanics and I was genuinely curious if it would make the game more or less fun.
