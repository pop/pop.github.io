Another great "meet the classic villians -- with a twist" Batman series.
Similar to "The Long Halloween", maybe one notch below on the tier list.

The ultimate bad guy is a complex mastermind and a good addition to the roster and I am excited to read more stories with him included.

The romance between Batman and Cat Woman is... interesting.
I like that a romantic relationship a new _type_ of relationship for Batman which forced him to be vulnerable.
Did that relationship _have_ to be romantic to achieve the same vulnerable result? Probably not.
Was it worse _because_ it was romantic? Honestly no. It was fine.
