It's a super silly show with very low stakes and a pretty good and consistent pace, making it easy to row to.

I do wish it kept going after season 3.
I could basically watch this show forever, in the same way I can watch _content_ like TikTok forever.
It scratches an itch, it's fun to watch, but honeslty I couldn't tell you what happened in any of the episodes...
