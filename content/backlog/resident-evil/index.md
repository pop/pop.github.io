Resident Evil was such a fun movie!

I really thought this nailed the game adaptation in a way that many other movies overlook; that being the "gamey scenario" screenplay.
Not just playing homage to the games, references to the source material, but putting the protagonist in a situation that as a gamer I thought "Oh he's gonna need that ladder..." or "Oh he should look for a key...".

As a screenplay I thought it was hilarious how literally nothing went right for the main character.
He hits a [zombie] person, gets pulled over by a cop, shoots his own car, hops an electric fence, gets chased by a [zombie] dog, and that's all just in the first like... 30 minutes!

The tone shift about 45 minutes in was... interesting.
It almost felt like smashing two movies together into one, but I also haven't played a Resident Evil game in full _ever_ so I may just be overlooking something.

I hope they do a sequel in a similar tone, it was a blast.
