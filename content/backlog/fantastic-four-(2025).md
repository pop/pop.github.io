I walked out of Fant4stic (2015) so the bar was pretty low.
The cast was totally solid, the story worked for me, the visuals felt fresh for a Marvel movie.

My only critique -- and it's not a negative just a note -- is like... the dialog felt off.
Like the direction for every line was "This is _the most important thing ever said_".
