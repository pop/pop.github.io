Orange World is a series of... mystical realism(?) short stories.

I listened to the audio-book of Orange World and thought it was exceptional.
The voice actors for each story played their characters very well, enhancing the experience.

A few stories I enjoyed:

* Bog Girl, about a boy's first love in a world where people are _way too ok_ with necrophilia.
* A story about a tornado rustler raising his last storm.
* A woman in post-flood south-eastern USA who can echo-locate like a bat.
* A demon blackmailing a new mother into giving it breast milk like a little freak.

I haven't read much of her work, but based on this I'd say Karen Russell is a great author.
I'm excited to read more of her stuff.
