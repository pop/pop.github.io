This one felt like the sequel to Godzilla Against Mechagodzilla.

It's also setting in that Godzilla used to be very pro-Japan, and every problem was solved by Japan just being an industry powerhouse.
Now it's at least Japan-skeptical with the industrious power being mighty but flawed with Mechagodzilla being a thing that Japan built but which causes about as many issues as it solves.

The only thing I can think of after _just watching it a few minutes ago_ is the after-credit scene is pretty tight.
