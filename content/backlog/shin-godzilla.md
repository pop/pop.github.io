Ok so I know I've said some of the other Godzillas were _good_ and they are -- each Godzilla is good in it's own way -- but Shin Godzilla is just categorically good.
Not "Good for a Godzilla" movie, not "Good for a 1980s monster movie" just a really good movie.

That 10 year hiatus between [Final Wars](/backlog/godzilla-final-wars/) and this really paid off.
* The pacing is impeccable, feeling at times like a West Wing episode with just how much happens in dialog alone.
* The visual effects are perfect, selling Godzilla's scale in a way that we haven't seen in a Toho production before.

I have no idea what the story is behind this movie but I am so glad it exists.
It doesn't feel like any other Godzilla movie, it looks so good, and it is firing on absolutely every cylinder!

---

I guess it does feel _like_ other Godzilla movies in that they do invent a novel interesting thing to finally kill Godzilla, but that's only a similarity on paper.
The actual moment to moment story telling, camera work, visual language -- all of that is totally different from anything I've ever seen in a Toho film.
