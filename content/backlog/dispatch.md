I was a fan of the TellTale "The Walking Dead" game whenever that came out.
I initially ignored Dispatch though because honestly I just have enough games to play.
Thankfully, a friend of mine played and really enjoyed Dispatch so I was pressured by my FOMO to play too.

The characters are great, the voice acting is fun, and the "gameplay" parts are really challenging (for me).

The biggest endorsement I can give is that if a sequel is announced I will pre-order it.
I never pre-order games.
