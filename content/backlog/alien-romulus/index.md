I thoroughly enjoyed Alien Romulus.
I, sadly, have not seen the original Alien in a loooong time, but Romulus does feel like it pays heavy homage to the source material.
From the grungy remote mining colony, to the utilitarian ships, everything felt dirty in the best possible way.

It's mix of practical and cgi visual effects pays off too.
Certain shots are undoubtedly CGI, like when an alien floats in zero gravity and it's blood pools around it in zero-g.
While others are obviously practical, like a face-hugger scurrying on the ground.
That said the multitude of shots in between, where the line is blurrier between what should be practical vs CG, is where the movie really gets it's money's worth.

> I saw a Visual Effects Artists React about this movie and they specifically pointed to a scene with a rat suspended in zero-g and then falling to the ground as a shot that looks incredible, was nearly impossible to do practically, yet sold perfectly.

All that aside, the real winner in this whole show was the body horror.
Oh my god, the body horror.

One scene a character freezes their fingers, ouch, that's bad.
Another scene you get a close up of a character having a face hugger... shlong... slipped out of their throat -- I'm throwing up with them.
But those are both just the bare minimum.
You expect that from an Alien movie.

The real body horror is when a chest burster is shown through x-rays in somebody's chest, and you see the ribs breaking. FUCK.

I'm sure there were more examples, but that one _still_ stands out without spoiling things more than I need to.

This movie was wonderful.
I am excited to see what other fun and interesting movies Ridley Scott can _produce_ but not you know... make himself.
