I got weirdly into Solitaire this last month.

It started with downloading the Microsoft Solitaire app on my phone.
I grew up playing Spider Solitaire on Windows XP, so I have some nostalgia for that.

Turns out there is _more than just Spider Solitaire_.
Klondike, Freecell, Pyramid, Tri Peaks, all very difficult!

Then I kept hearing about the Zachtronics Solitaire Collection and thought "Sure, that guy seems pretty smart".
And those are also really difficult!
But there is no undo button so they kick my ass even harder!

Solitaire is a fun and interesting genre of game.
I'd like to make one someday.
