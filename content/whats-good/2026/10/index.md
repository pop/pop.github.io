I slept on Barbarian, even after watching Weapons when it came out.

# [The House That Jack Built (2018)](/backlog/the-house-that-jack-built)

This is classic Rotten media, and like a lot of Rotten movies, books, games, etc., it has a great pay-off at the end with some genuinely beautiful scenes.

# [I Saw the TV Glow (2024)](/backlog/i-saw-the-tv-glow)

An immaculate "vibes movie" and a really good metaphor for the struggle with being trans/transitioning/fighting it.

# [Late Night with the Devil (2023)](/backlog/late-night-with-the-devil)

A nice bottle episode movie with some satanic panic, analogue horror, and a fun premise.
