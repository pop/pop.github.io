Jaden Williams' The Stalking Stairs

published 2026-11-09 | tags: #games #backlog

critiques:

  • end boss was not well constructed, took me finding a bug to discover the solution
  • visual aesthetic is wonky. i think he's going for a ps1 asthetic but it doesn't quite work. that said it is unique so... cool?
  • let me skip cutscenes!
  • controller support please!

it's interesting to play a game by a talented person in one domain (youtube/tiktok sketches) make a game. clearly a lot of work went into this, but its also very clearly a "my first published game, and I'm a solo developer" project. don't get me wrong, the first game i end up publishing on steam will likely be as good or worse, so i am not shitting on this, just being realistic.

it's not the best horror game out there, and obviously if you like Jaden's work and you want to support him you should check it out. That said I wouldn't suggest this to somebody who is a fan of horror as it doesn't do anything especially novel, and what it does it doesn't do especially well.

I'm interested to follow his game making career, i think he has another game in production. i also hope he leans on other creatives to make a better end product, in a shorter time, at the cost of some contractor salaries.
