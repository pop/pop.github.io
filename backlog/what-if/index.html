What If...? (2021 - 2024)

published 2026-01-31 | tags: #backlog #tv

I watched Seasons 2 and 3 of What If while rowing this month. It's a super silly show with very low stakes and a pretty good and consistent pace, making it easy to row to.

I do wish it kept going after season 3. I could basically watch this show forever, in the same way I can watch content like TikTok forever. It scratches an itch, it's fun to watch, but honeslty I couldn't tell you what happened in any of the episodes...
