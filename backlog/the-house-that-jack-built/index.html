The House That Jack Built (2018)

published 2026-10-27 | tags: #backlog #movies

In the spirit of Halloween, I've been watching horror movies. I picked this one from my "to watch" list sort of at random, sort of based on the post.

I went in with no expectations and was surprised to find a truly rotten movie, which was a pleasant surprise.

I'm not sure what else to add. I really like Rotten media, as a subgenre of Horror, but I haven't figured out why I like it more than other horror.

Yes the main character is a piece of shit, yes he kills people, don't worry he gets what he deserves.

I especially like the visuals at the end where Jack... let's say he goes on a journey. It's a very painterly scene.
