Batman: Hush (2003)

published 2025-09-26 | tags: #backlog #comics

Another great "meet the classic villians -- with a twist" Batman series. Similar to "The Long Halloween", maybe one notch below on the tier list.

The ultimate bad guy is a complex mastermind and a good addition to the roster and I am excited to read more stories with him included.

The romance between Batman and Cat Woman is... interesting. I like that a romantic relationship a new type of relationship for Batman which forced him to be vulnerable. Did that relationship have to be romantic to achieve the same vulnerable result? Probably not. Was it worse because it was romantic? Honestly no. It was fine.
