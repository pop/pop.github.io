Godzilla vs Kong

published 2025-12-11 | tags: #backlog #movies #godzilla

Gozilla vs Kong (vs MechaGozilla) feels like a direct continuation of Godzilla: King of the Monsters, weaker in some respects and stronger in others.

Pros:

  • As a neon-stan, the color scheme is incredible.
  • The fight in Hong Kong is epic.
  • MechaGodzilla looks badass, and the team-up is fun.

Cons:

  • A weaker cast than King of the Monsters
  • Not enough monsters, especially compared with King of the Monsters

Somewhere in the middle:

  • The story and acting just felt sillier
  • Wow they really ran with that "Hollow Earth" thing

Over all a totally pleasent way to spend 2 hours, but also not a "must watch".
